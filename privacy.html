Your project. Your privacy.

When you request an estimate, Under One Roof Services LLC receives the name, email, phone number, ZIP code and project details you submit. This information is used to respond to your request, discuss your project and arrange your estimate.

The price calculator runs in your browser. Your calculation and selected color are included with your request only when you submit the estimate form. The floor visualizer processes selected photos on your device and does not send those photos with your request. Saved color favorites use your browser’s local storage.

When hosted on Netlify with form detection enabled, Netlify processes form submissions for Under One Roof. The hosting provider may also process technical information needed to serve and secure the website. This page does not include advertising trackers.
