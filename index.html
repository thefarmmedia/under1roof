A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Good questions.
Straight answers.

How much does a concrete coating cost?

Decorative flake is estimated at $6–$8 per square foot, and metallic at $14–$16 per square foot. Use the calculator for a planning range. We confirm the final price after measuring and inspecting your concrete.

Do you offer financing?

Yes, financing is available. Ask our team about current options and applicable terms during your estimate. This page does not quote monthly payments or financing rates.

What does the 25-year warranty cover?

Qualifying coating systems include a 25-year written warranty. We’ll provide the terms, coverage and any exclusions for your project before installation.

How long does installation take?

Many coating projects can be completed in one day. The schedule and return-to-use time depend on the concrete, weather and chosen system. Your team will confirm both before work starts.

Will my floor look exactly like the visualizer?

The visualizer is a digital color preview. Lighting, screen settings and hand-applied finishes affect the final appearance. Confirm your selection with a physical sample.

Can you coat patios and commercial spaces?

Yes. We work on garages, patios, pool decks, driveways and commercial concrete. We’ll recommend a suitable coating after reviewing how you use the space.
